Station 4: Digital Universe Area – Print! Passwords! Pods!

This is the tech heart of the library—and a digital playground for self-driven learners.

If you’re a Yuan Ze faculty member or student, log in with your Portal account to unlock access to dozens of computers ready for research, reports, video editing, and more.

For campus visitors, standing kiosks are available —just a few taps and you can search the catalog or look up resources. Friendly and super easy to use!

Need to print something? No problem—EasyCard makes it a breeze.

Just set your printing password, insert your card, tap to print, and you’re done in three simple steps. Fast and convenient.

Need a spot to collaborate with others or focus solo?

Our group study rooms and individual study pods have you covered. Whether you’re brainstorming ideas, rehearsing a presentation, hopping on a voice call, or recording a podcast, these spaces are your go-to companions.

Here, technology lights the path and learning never takes a break.
