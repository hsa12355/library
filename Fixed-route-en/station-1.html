Station 1: Smart Library Wall — Can a Wall Recommend Books by Looking at Your Face?

In a certain corner of the library, there’s a magical wall said to recommend books… just by looking at your face.

That’s right! It’s the “Smart Library Wall,” born in December 2018.

With three powerful functions—eBook Picks by Theme, Grab It with a Scan, and Snap-to-Read Technology—it’s enthusiastic, proactive, and full of high-tech charm!

Some say it’s like the AI Cupid of the reading world, using facial recognition to match you with your soulmate book.

Even the university president couldn’t resist trying it out on opening day—he snapped a photo, got a copy of Life Is Like a Storybook, and kicked off his winter reading adventure right then and there.

Scan, download, snap, read—this is where technology and reading fall into a high-profile romance.

And now, our own journey begins, heading deeper into the heart of the library to uncover more wondrous scenes...
