Station 7: Current Periodicals – Catching Rays with Your Reads

Welcome to the main stage of journals—where the latest Chinese and international periodicals are gathered, from current affairs to design inspiration.

Lined up like a mini world expo of knowledge, they’re waiting to be explored.

And the best part? The beloved annual “Journal Sunbathing” event—where the library clears out past issues and invites readers to take them home, no strings attached.

Academic content once considered niche becomes creative gold—ideal for artistic collage, interior décor, or simply adding character to your shelf.

Most periodicals are for in-library use only, but the immersive feeling of reading in this space is so good, you won’t want to leave.

But now, let’s soften the pace a bit and revisit the imagination of our childhoods.

When was the last time you picked up a picture book?

Let’s go rediscover that quiet sense of wonder.
