Station 11: Magic Forest – Where Knowledge Begins

If this whole journey were a poem, this final stop would be its most powerful closing line.

This green oasis in the central courtyard isn’t just trees—it’s a living metaphor for knowledge itself. Trees become paper. Paper becomes books. Books become bridges to our future.

Here, you’ll find TOEIC collection, the “50 Must-Read Classics”, and showcases of student creativity.

It’s a space where language, critical thinking, and art come together—a tiny world of expression and discovery.

Having wandered through forests and book walls, paused in a Zen garden, and stepped into tech-powered study pods, we’ve done much more than just tour a library.
