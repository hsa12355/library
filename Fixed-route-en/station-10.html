Station 10: Japanese Zen Garden – Stillness in Motion

There’s no water here, yet you can sense a flowing stream.

No mountains in sight, but a quiet, elevated calm surrounds you.

Welcome to the library’s inner sanctuary—the Japanese rock garden, or karesansui.

The sand patterns mimic flowing water, while carefully placed stones evoke silent mountain peaks.

Everything seems still, yet within that silence, there’s subtle movement. In that stillness, thoughts ripple gently.

President Ching-Jong Liao once recorded a special video message here, using this garden to symbolize the quiet strength students build throughout their university journey.

This space is more than just a landscape—it’s a haven for thought, reflection, and calm.

And now, we arrive at our final stop—the root of knowledge and nature. A forest. But not just any forest—a magical one.
