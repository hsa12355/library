Station 2: Entrance – The Door to Knowledge Opens Here

Every journey begins with a door.

The library’s smart entrance system is like a gateway into the universe of knowledge. Just a tap, and your reading adventure begins.

Swipe your student ID or launch the access page on the Yuan Ze app, and the smart door slides open quietly. Suddenly, you’re not just walking into a library—you’re embarking on a story-filled adventure of knowledge.

The library originally opened its doors in 1989, relocated to its current home in 1997, and underwent a major expansion in 2006. Today, it spans a modern 10,000-square-meter space, brimming with knowledge and rich memories. Each of its three floors has its own distinct personality:

• B1 feels like a tranquil forest of books, with book stacks, cozy discussion rooms, and a calming Japanese Zen garden.

• The 1st floor serves as the vibrant hub for all information and services.

• The 2nd floor is a reader’s paradise, bursting with journals, picture books, and manga.

Ready to dive in? Let’s head over to the freshest battleground of books—the New TITLES!
