Station 9: Compact Shelves Area – Spin the Wheel of Knowledge

Welcome to the library’s secret backstage—a treasure trove of lesser-used yet surprisingly wonderful books.

Don’t be fooled by the calm—it’s full of motion when you need it!

Each shelf is mobile. Just turn the wheel on the side, and the bookshelf slides open like a gateway, clearing a path as if knowledge itself is inviting you in.

Searching through it feels like a mini quest—like you’re solving a puzzle or exploring hidden ruins.

But remember: this isn’t a game room. It’s a labyrinth of knowledge.

As you wander deeper, stay focused—we’re about to enter the calmest, most meditative part of the entire library.
