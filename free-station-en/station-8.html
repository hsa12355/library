Station 8: Picture Books & Comics – SDGs Adventures in Storyland

Welcome to the imagination paradise for kids and grown-ups alike.

From heartwarming picture books to adrenaline-packed manga, this cozy, colorful corner lets stories speak loud and clear.

Right at the heart of it all is a full shelf dedicated to the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Topics like climate change, the environment, and Earth’s future might sound complex, but here they come alive through vivid illustrations and meaningful tales.

Some stories will make you laugh. Others might bring tears to your eyes.

But every one of them teaches us how kindness and storytelling can help protect our world.
